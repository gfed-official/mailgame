## Acceptance Criteria

*From GDD PCM-P5-2, PCM-P5-4, PCM-P5-5, PCM-P4-1d (game-designer rulings 2026-10-16) and TD-091 (2)–(3). All Vy figures below are the **launch speed set by the rule**. The shipped convention (tests `P5_ExtraJump_SetsVerticalSpeedTo6830_AtAnyFallSpeed`, `F3_Jump_FiresOnThePressTick_AndThatTickIntegratesWithV0`) is that a launch on the press or landing tick also integrates that tick, so the observed post-tick `Vy` is the launch speed minus `g/30` (800): 6030 for the extra jump and 6789 for the ground jump. The GDD's "Vy 6830 / 7589" means the launch speed. The one exception is the expiry fallback (AC 5), where the GDD text fixes the post-tick `Vy` at 6830 exactly. Tests must state which of the two they assert. Fixtures: `WorldTablesFixture.Dry()` (flat 0), a `RampWorld(1404)` style 702 ‰ face (`SweepTerrainTests`), static boxes via `ColliderTests` helpers.*

- [ ] **PCM-P5-5 (key range).** `player.maxExtraJumps` keeps default 1 but its hard range becomes 0–1 (`BalanceKeys.Player.cs` knob, currently `new BalanceKnob(MaxExtraJumpsKey, BalanceUnit.Count, 1, 0, 16)` at line 209). A data file with `maxExtraJumps: 2` builds with a `CV-GEN-13` **error** (hard-range violation, raised by `BalanceTable.Build`) and no `ContentSet`; 0 and 1 build. With `maxExtraJumps 1` an `ExtraJumps` stat of 3 grants exactly one mid-air jump (the `StatDefs` upper bound `StatBound.Balance(MaxExtraJumps)` now resolves to 1, and `CarrierJump.ExtraAvailable` already applies `min(ExtraJumps, MaxExtraJumps) >= 1`); with `maxExtraJumps 0` none.
- [ ] **`landProbeMm` (F3).** A pure, public, doc-commented `CarrierJump.LandProbeMm(vy, settings)` returns `max(0, (n² × (g/30) − 2 × n × vy) / 60)` with `n = jumpBufferTicks + 1`, in `long`, one truncating division of a non-negative numerator. At the defaults (n = 5, g/30 = 800): vy 0 → 333; −3000 → 833; −7589 → 1598; −15 000 → 2833; +2000 → 0; +6830 → 0 (clamped, never negative). It reads `n` and `g` from `PlayerSettings` (test with `jumpBufferTicks` 0 and 6).
- [ ] **PCM-P5-4 (press resolution, parametrised).** GIVEN one extra jump (`ExtraJumps` 1, flag clear), Airborne, no coyote, `Vy` −3000 at tick start over flat walkable ground, the press tick is T:
  - feet 800 mm up (≤ `landProbeMm` 833): no mid-air jump on T (`ExtraJumpUsed` still clear, `JumpBufferLeft` = 4 after the arm), and the landing tick fires the **ground** jump (launch 7589, post-tick `Vy` 6789) with the extra jump unused and reset by the landing. Derive the landing tick by hand from the F3 trapezoid and assert it falls inside the 4-tick buffer window;
  - feet 900 mm up (> 833): extra jump on T (launch 6830, post-tick `Vy` 6030, `ExtraJumpUsed` set);
  - a press while rising (`Vy` +2000, feet 100 mm up): `landProbeMm` is 0, so the extra jump fires on T;
  - a press with coyote left (`CoyoteLeft` > 0, feet 800 mm up): the **ground** jump at v0 7589, not the extra jump (step 1 of the order beats step 2);
  - over a 702 ‰ face 100 mm below the feet: not near support (steep terrain is never near support), extra jump on T;
  - a press with **no** extra jump available (flag set, or `ExtraJumps` 0) near support or not: unchanged shipped behaviour — the press arms the buffer and a landing inside the window fires the ground jump.
  Near support means `CarrierSweep.GroundResolve` at the press tick's **start** position `(X, Y, Z)` (after `EntryGround`, before gravity) returns `walkable` and `Y − support ≤ landProbeMm`; a support above the feet (embedded start) counts as near.
- [ ] **Expiry fallback (PCM-P5-4, 3rd vector).** A press deferred by the near-support rule while the extra jump was available, whose support is then removed (box top deleted from the world, or the carrier drifts off the ledge) so the carrier is still Airborne when the buffer ages 1 → 0 (the end of tick T+4), fires the extra jump at that moment: after tick T+4, `Vy` = 6830 exactly, `RemY` = 0, `ExtraJumpUsed` set, `JumpBufferLeft` = 0, no integration on that tick (the arc starts on T+5). It does **not** fire if the carrier landed on T+4 or earlier (the ground jump fired instead), if the context became Panel/Dead/Mounted or the carrier is Swimming (the buffer was cleared by `Arm`), or if the extra jump was not available when the press was made.
- [ ] **PCM-P4-1d (steep-slope slide, parametrised).** New pure, doc-commented helper (suggest `CarrierSlide`-adjacent `CarrierTerrain.SlideStep` or a new `CarrierSteepSlide`, implementer's choice, same folder) computes, from the contact point's un-divided gradient numerators (`gx`, `gz`, as `GroundResolve` returns them): `S2 = gxPm² + gzPm²` (permille², exact in `Int128`, not from a rounded `S`), `S = isqrt(S2)`, `aSlide = g × S2 / (S2 + 1 000 000)` (one floor division), `vyCap = min(terminalMmS, slideRunMaxMmS × S / 1000)`, `vy' = max(min(vy, 0) − aSlide / 30, −vyCap)` where `vy` is `Vy` at tick start. On a contact tick `Vy` becomes `vy'` (**no longer zeroed**), `drop = min(sunk depth, FloorDiv-trapezoid of (min(vy, 0), vy') with RemY)`, and the horizontal run is `CarrierTerrain.DownhillRun(gx, gz, drop)`. From rest on a 702 ‰ face (S2 492 804): `aSlide` 7922; `Vy` −264 after tick 1, −2640 after tick 10, −2808 (the cap) from tick 11 on; 93–94 mm down and 133–134 mm along the gradient per capped tick (4000 mm/s). 1000 ‰: `aSlide` 12 000, cap −4000 reached on tick 10. 3000 ‰: cap −12 000. 5000 ‰: cap = terminal −20 000. Reaching walkable ground at the foot lands normally with horizontal velocity unchanged. A sliding carrier is Airborne: a live coyote jump or the extra jump works, a ground jump does not.
- [ ] **PCM-P4-1d (vs. shipped code).** The shipped `CarrierSweep.PressIntoWall` (lines 416–451) zeroes `Vy` and `RemY` (lines 446–447) and derives the run from the full sunk depth. After this story no zeroing remains; a fall at −20 000 onto a 702 ‰ face is capped to −2808 on the contact tick (`min(vy, 0) − aSlide/30` is below the cap, so the cap wins), and `Vy` persists into the air if the face ends in a drop.
- [ ] **`player.slideRunMaxMmS`.** New balance key, default 4000, `BalanceUnit.MmPerSec`, safe range 2500–5000 (outside it: `CV-GEN-20` warning, still builds), hard range 1–100 000 (story decision: the GDD gives only the safe range; the hard range mirrors sibling mm/s knobs, see Open Questions). Bound through `BalanceKeys.Player.cs` (key const, knob, handle), `PlayerBalance` (record field, property, `FromTable`), `PlayerSettings` (property + `FromBalance`) and read by the sweep as `settings.SlideRunMaxMmS`; the "one property per balance key" test in `PlayerSettingsTests` passes, and `BuiltInContentTests` pins `("player.slideRunMaxMmS", BalanceUnit.MmPerSec, 4000)`. Changing it to 2500 and 5000 changes the cap per the formula (2500 × 702 / 1000 = 1755; 5000 × 702 / 1000 = 3510).
- [ ] **ContentHash golden.** Adding the key changes the M1a content bytes: `ContentHashTests.GoldenM1aDigest` and `GoldenM1aDisplay` (`tests/unit/content/ContentHashTests.cs` lines 30–32) are regenerated in this story's commit following the class remarks (run `ContentHash_M1a_MatchesTheGoldenDigest`, read the new digest from the failure message, confirm the only content change is the new key and the `maxExtraJumps` hard-range change if it is hashed, then paste). ## Implementation Notes

*Derived from the GDD rulings and the code as read on 2026-10-17. Where TD-091's wording differs from the code, this section says what is actually there.*

- **Hook map (verified).**
  - `CarrierGravity.Apply` (`CarrierGravity.cs:82`) calls `CarrierJump.Arm` (line 87), runs `EntryGround` (line 103), then `AirborneTick` (lines 125–142) and `CarrierJump.AgeCounters` (line 120). The extra jump today fires on `armedNow && ExtraAvailable` with no support test (lines 133–138); that branch gains `&& !NearSupport(...)`. `NearSupport` calls `CarrierSweep.GroundResolve` once at `state.X/Y/Z` (the tick start position, after `EntryGround`, before gravity) and `CarrierJump.LandProbeMm`. When the extra jump is available but the press is near support, `pending` stays true so the sweep's landing path (`VerticalAndGround` → `LaunchOnLanding`) fires the ground jump, as for any buffered press.
  - `CarrierJump.Arm` itself needs no change (it sets `JumpBufferLeft = jumpBufferTicks` on an allowed press and clears the buffer on a disallowed context). `CarrierJump.ExtraAvailable` needs no change; the 0–1 cap comes from the key range.
  - **Expiry fallback timing.** `AgeCounters` is called at the end of `Apply`, i.e. **before** the sweep (phase 5). The GDD requires the extra jump to fire at the end of tick T+4 with `Vy` = 6830 and no integration on that tick, which must also happen only if the sweep did not land the carrier. So the expiry check cannot live in the current `AgeCounters` call. Recommended: keep `AgeCounters` where it is, but make `JumpBufferLeft` aging for an Airborne carrier with the extra jump available a post-sweep step (a new `CarrierJump.ExpireDeferred(ref state, in input, settings)` called from `OwnedCarrier.Step` after `Sweep`, only when `state.Loco == Airborne`, `JumpBufferLeft == 1` at the start of the aging, not `armedNow`, and `ExtraAvailable`). It then sets `Vy = v0Extra`, `RemY = 0`, `ExtraJumpUsed`, `JumpBufferLeft = 0`. Whatever the structure, `JumpBufferLeft` at the start of the next tick must be unchanged for every non-expiring case (the `P5_BufferedPress_*` tests in `JumpTests.cs` lines 241–310 must stay green untouched). `GravityPlan` already crosses the phase boundary and may carry a flag if the implementer prefers.
  - **Slide.** `CarrierSweep.VerticalAndGround` (lines 334–386) calls `PressIntoWall` (line 369) when the support is not walkable. `PressIntoWall` already receives the un-divided gradient numerators `gx`, `gz`. It needs the tick-start `Vy` and `RemY`, which phase 4 has already overwritten with the unclamped integration, so `GravityPlan` gains `VyStart` and `RemYStart` (set in `Apply` after any launch, before `IntegrateState`; for a Grounded carrier that did not launch they are 0). `PressIntoWall` then computes `vy'` and the trapezoid per AC "PCM-P4-1d", writes `state.Vy = vy'` and `state.RemY` (replacing lines 446–447), and uses `drop` instead of `depth` for `DownhillRun`. `CarrierTerrain.DownhillRun` (`CarrierTerrain.cs:79`) already computes `depth × 1000 / S` along the gradient from the numerators and needs no change.
  - **Numerators to permille.** The numerators are un-divided (S = 2000 mm tile): a 702 ‰ face has `gx` 2 808 000. Convert exactly in `Int128` (`S2 = gxPm² + gzPm²` with `gPm = g × 1000 / 2000²`, or equivalently `S2 = (gx² + gz²) × 1000² / 2000⁴`) so that `S2` for 1404/2000 is 492 804 and `aSlide` is 7922. Pin that conversion with the 702 ‰, 1000 ‰, 3000 ‰ and 5000 ‰ vectors.
  - **Carrying `RemY` when the sunk depth limits the drop (GDD silent).** This story carries `RemY = n − 60 × dyTrapezoid` exactly as `CarrierGravity.Integrate` does, regardless of whether `drop` was reduced to the sunk depth. The effect is sub-millimetre; recorded in Open Questions for ratification.
  - **Multi-sub-step contact.** The sunk depth is bounded by the contacting sub-step's vertical share while the trapezoid is the whole tick's; `min(sunk, trapezoid)` covers both. The rest of the tick's vertical displacement is spent on contact (`verticalSpent`), as now.
- **Settings and content.** Add `SlideRunMaxMmS` everywhere `SlopeMaxPm` is plumbed: `BalanceKeys.Player.cs` (const near line 146, knob near line 213, handle near line 282), `PlayerBalance.cs` (record parameter near line 49, property near line 198, binding near line 271), `PlayerSettings.cs` (property near line 155, `FromBalance` near line 230), `PlayerSettingsTests.GddDefaults` (near line 60), `BuiltInContentTests` pinned list (near line 466), `ContentRuleTestKit.cs` defaults map (line 201). `MaxExtraJumpsKey` knob: change `0, 16` to `0, 1`.
- **Files** in `src/PerformativeMail.Sim/`: `Players/CarrierJump.cs` (`LandProbeMm`, `NearSupport`, expiry step), `Players/CarrierGravity.cs` (`AirborneTick`, `GravityPlan.VyStart/RemYStart`), `Players/CarrierSweep.cs` (`PressIntoWall`, `Run` swimmer delegation), `Players/OwnedCarrier.cs` (expiry call, optional swimmer-branch removal), `Players/CarrierWater.cs` (line 172; sink threading at line 132), `Players/CarrierHaulOut.cs` (lines 119, 136–139), `Players/CarrierTerrain.cs` or a new `Players/CarrierSteepSlide.cs` (slide maths), `Players/PlayerSettings.cs`, `Content/Balance/BalanceKeys.Player.cs`, `Content/Balance/PlayerBalance.cs`. Tests in `tests/unit/player/` and `tests/unit/content/`, `tests/integration/content/` as listed in the ACs.

This story pins the current behaviour (depth 0 on any box top) and does not decide. Owner: game-designer. Raised in story 009 (Deviation 4) and TD-091(2).

Assumed the GDD means launch speed (and exactly 6830 only for the expiry fallback). Confirm so the GDD text can be tightened.
