## Acceptance Criteria

*From GDD `design/gdd/throwing-catching.md` Feel & Comedy, Game Feel, Visual/Audio and TC-FE-2, scoped to M-1a (ground, water, solid, timeout, reject-bounce landings; mailbox door reactions are Mail's):*

- [ ] **Ghost leaves the hand on the release frame (TC-FE-2).** On the frame the Throw commit is sent (`ReleaseRecord`, story 007) the ghost mesh is visible at the hand socket and moves on that frame; over a synthetic replay corpus `tests/replay/throwing/` (≥ 200 releases, mouse and pad profiles, generated by a seeded script) the ghost's first drawn frame minus the release frame is ≤ 1 frame for every release; the parcel is never delayed for any animation state.
- [ ] **Ghost behaviour on screen.** The view draws story 017's `LaunchGhost` output: lerp onto the F1 path in 3 ticks, lead the authoritative flight, blend in time on `ThrowLaunched`, snap on `originClamped`, hold at a static contact (≤ 600 ms) until the outcome event, fade 150 ms on rejection. No hovering parcel remains at a mailbox rim after the outcome (manual check at ≥ 120 ms simulated one-way delay through the loopback `DelayTransport`).
- [ ] **Strain and snap.** While `CarrierStatus.Aiming`, the face rig shows the **strain** cell and the carry pose raises to the wind-up; at the release frame the throw one-shot snaps the arm through (art bible §5 grammar: squash 0.2 s → pop ≤ 0.1 s → hold ≥ 0.15 s; limbs stretch ≤ 1.3×) and the face returns to neutral. An early release or cancel drops the arm and clears the strain without a throw. The animation is driven from published state; the rig never gates input or movement.
- [ ] **Whoosh and cancel cues.** `sfx_throw_release` (3D, all peers) is issued on `ThrowLaunched` for every peer (and at the ghost spawn for the thrower), pitched 0 / −2 / −4 / −6 semitones by bundle tier (1 / 2–3 / 4–9 / 10+ units), ≤ 6 voices and 80 ms per thrower; `sfx_throw_cancel` (2D, thrower) on a cancelled aim. The cue rate limiters are pure and unit-tested; the Audio presenter is a port (`IAudioCueSink`) because no audio-presenter story exists — a null sink is a counted no-op.
- [ ] **Landing beats are outcome beats.** For GROUND, solid, TIMEOUT and MAILBOX_REJECT / floor-remainder bounces the thud (`sfx_land_ground_s/m/l` by weight class), the squash (0.8–1.2 scale, 0.2 s → pop ≤ 0.1 s → hold ≥ 0.15 s) and a dust puff (pooled, ≤ 8) play at the `FlightResolved.contact` / drop point, and for WATER the plop (`sfx_land_water`) and a foam ring (pooled) at the contact point; the landed parcel lands glyph-up. The beat controllers are tagged `[OutcomeBeat]` and read only `PresentationEventStream` cursors; the ghost and predictive components expose no landing callback.
- [ ] **Never before the event.** An integration test through the loopback `DelayTransport` (fixed 3–10 tick delays) records every landing-beat start frame and asserts each is ≥ the arrival frame of its `FlightResolved`, and also ≥ the frame at which the flight visual reaches the contact (story 020's landed query), for ≥ 200 randomised landings; a beat never plays for a flight whose event never arrives.
- [ ] **Merged landing sfx.** Landing sfx are limited to 4 per 250 ms merged by position (pure limiter, unit-tested); the mailbox teeter, flag and door reactions are Mail's (`production/epics/mail-destinations/story-016-mailbox-greybox-view-outcome-beats.md`) and are not duplicated here.
- [ ] **No screen shake by default** and none after a landing; no hit-stop on the player or camera (Later: the optional heavy-landing shake setting).
- [ ] **0 B / pools.** 600 frames with 8 simultaneous landings and one ghost allocate 0 managed bytes in this story's views; all pooled nodes exist after boot.
- [ ] **Visual check (manual).** A scripted fixture records a light single to the ground, a 20-stack across the street, a splash into the sea and a closed-mailbox bounce; the capture shows the strain face, the snap, the whoosh marker, and the squash / dust / foam beats at the right moments; the lead has the evidence sign-off.

## Implementation Notes

*Derived from ADR-0016 §5 and GDD Visual/Audio:*

- Files in `src/Game/Views/Throwing/`: `LaunchGhostView.cs` (a pooled mesh node driven by `LaunchGhost`), `ThrowRigDriver.cs` (sets AnimationTree parameters / face cell from `CarrierStatus.Aiming` and `ReleaseRecord`; extends `production/epics/player-character/story-024-animationtree-greybox-rig-gait-spike.md`'s state machine through its extension point — coordinate, do not fork the tree), `LandingBeatController.cs` (`[OutcomeBeat]`), `LandingPools.cs` (dust, foam ring), and in `src/PerformativeMail.Client.Core/Throwing/`: `ThrowAudioCues.cs` (pure limiters), `IAudioCueSink.cs`.
- The hand socket is a node on the PlayerView rig (`production/epics/player-character/story-022-playerview-scene-render-pose.md`); expose it via the view registry, never via the Sim. The release snap frame is the `Present` that receives the new `ReleaseRecord`.
- The catch one-shot, the hands-up pose, the FORWARDED stamp, the bonk hat-lift and the fumble beat are M0 and not built here; keep the one-shot slot names reserved in the AnimationTree only if story 024's tree has them.
- Audio assets: the cue ids in the GDD are proposals for the audio-director; until assets exist the sink may log. Do not add placeholder audio files to the repository to make a test pass.
- The landing glyph-up orientation is the WorldItem view's (see the unowned `WorldItemView` seam in story 020 and EPIC.md); this story orients the **flight parcel** at the contact and hands off through `IFlightLandedQuery`.
